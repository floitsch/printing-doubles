Two ways to explain each algorithm

For every algorithm, two independent teams proposed ways to make it click. Here are the two strongest ideas, each built as a complete, self-contained page. Read both and keep the one that teaches better.

Each page assumes the floating-point primer: binary grid, ulp, the rounding interval between the midpoints, round-trip, shortest and closest. The existing chapter is linked under each pair for comparison.
